Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Who we are

Axiovex Systems, LLC ("Axiovex Systems," "we," "us," or "our") is a Michigan company. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit axiovexsystems.com (the "Site"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at legal@axiovexsystems.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide. The Site has no accounts. You can share personal information with us directly in three ways: by emailing us; through the contact form, which asks for your name, email address, topic, and message, and optionally your company; or by signing up for our newsletter with your email address (see the newsletter section below). The contact form delivers your message to our mailbox, and we do not store form submissions anywhere else. Both forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, a spam check run by Cloudflare (see the cookies and local storage section).

Information collected automatically. The Site is a static website hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most websites, our hosting provider processes limited technical data to deliver and protect the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. We review aggregate statistics derived from this technical data — for example, how many requests each page receives and which countries the requests come from — to operate the Site. Those statistics are computed from our hosting provider's server logs; no analytics script runs in your browser. We do not combine this data with any other personal information. When you submit a form, our anti-abuse rate limiting also records a one-way cryptographic hash of your IP address — never the address itself — so automated abuse can be slowed down.

What we do not collect. We do not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or other cross-site tracking technologies, and we do not set cookies of our own (the next section explains the full picture). We do not build profiles of visitors, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

3. Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies. This Site does not set cookies of its own, and nothing on the Site reads cookies. Because we set no cookies and run no optional tracking, there is no cookie banner and nothing to opt out of here.

The spam check on our forms. The contact form and the newsletter signup include a spam check called Turnstile, run by Cloudflare. The check runs in a frame served by Cloudflare, and while it runs Cloudflare may use its own strictly necessary storage on its own domain (challenges.cloudflare.com). That storage belongs to Cloudflare, exists only to tell people from automated bots, and is not used to track you across other websites. If you block it, the forms cannot verify a submission and will not send.

Local storage on your own device. A few features keep small pieces of information in your browser's local storage, on your device only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Whether you dismissed the breaking-news banner, so it stays dismissed;
  • For our audio editions, where you stopped listening — the chapter and the position, kept for seven days so you can pick up where you left off — and the playback speed and narrator voice you chose.

You can clear these at any time through your browser's site-data settings; the Site works without them. All of the Site's media, including the audio editions, is served from this Site itself, so no outside media service sees what you read or listen to here.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Operate, secure, and maintain the Site;
  • Respond to emails and inquiries you send us; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us operate the Site process information on our behalf and only as instructed by us: Cloudflare hosts the Site, runs the spam checks on our forms, and stores the newsletter subscriber list; Microsoft (Microsoft 365) provides our email, receives contact-form deliveries, and sends the newsletter from our own mailbox.
  • Legal requirements. If required by law, court order, or government request, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, visitors, or the public.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

6. Data retention

We keep email correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, then delete or archive it when it is no longer needed. Newsletter records are kept while you are subscribed; if you unsubscribe, we keep your address only on a suppression record so it cannot be re-added without a fresh confirmation (see the newsletter section). A newsletter confirmation link expires after 48 hours. Technical logs retained by our hosting provider are subject to that provider's own retention practices.

7. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information in our care, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the Site. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information:

  • California residents (under the CCPA/CPRA) have the right to know what personal information we hold about them, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
  • European Economic Area and UK visitors (under the GDPR/UK GDPR) have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing. Our lawful bases for processing are our legitimate interests in operating the Site and responding to inquiries, and compliance with legal obligations.
  • Other U.S. states with consumer privacy laws grant similar rights of access, correction, deletion, and opt-out of sales or targeted advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, email legal@axiovexsystems.com. We will verify your identity before responding and will respond within the time required by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-party links

The Site links to third-party websites and services, including LinkedIn, GitHub, our public course materials, and the websites of organizations we belong to. This policy does not apply to those sites; their own privacy policies govern your use of them.

11. Do Not Track

Because the Site uses no tracking technologies, there is nothing to disable in response to Do Not Track signals.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

13. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices: legal@axiovexsystems.com.

14. The Axiovex Signal newsletter

If you subscribe to The Axiovex Signal, our monthly newsletter, we collect your email address, your subscription status, the timestamps of your signup, confirmation, and any unsubscribe, and which signup form you used (for example, the one on the Signals page or in an article) — nothing else. We use that information only to send the newsletter you asked for. Subscribing requires a confirmation click (double opt-in); until you confirm, nothing is sent to your address.

Two processors handle this data for us: Microsoft (Microsoft 365 sends the newsletter from our own tenant mailbox) and Cloudflare (which hosts this Site and stores the subscriber list). We do not sell subscriber information, and we do not share it with anyone beyond those two processors.

The newsletter carries no tracking pixels and no per-reader analytics: we do not record whether you open an issue or click its links. We keep only aggregate counts — how many issues were sent, bounced, or complained about.

Every issue carries a one-click unsubscribe, and it takes effect immediately. When you unsubscribe, we keep your address on a suppression record — held so that the address cannot be silently re-added — and it is never used for any other purpose. Subscribing again is always possible, and always takes a fresh confirmation click.

Unsubscribing stops the emails. If you would rather have your record removed entirely — including from the suppression record — email legal@axiovexsystems.com and we will delete it.

15. Our Disclaimer

This policy covers personal information only. Reliance on the Site's published content — our analysis, the figures we republish, and the projections we show — is covered by our Disclaimer.
