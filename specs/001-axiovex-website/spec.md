**Feature Branch**: `001-axiovex-website`

**Created**: 2026-10-05 (baseline capture of the live site)

**Status**: Implemented and live — this spec records the as-built system so
every future change runs through the SDD flow against it.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Prospective client evaluates the firm (Priority: P1)

A manufacturing or organizational leader lands on the home page, reads the
method ("out of the box approach" / structural epistemic framework), the
three service lines, and the evidence band, and reaches Contact.

**Independent Test**: Walk Home → What we do → About → FAQ → Contact;
every claim must satisfy constitution §I (claims discipline).

### User Story 2 - Reader consumes analysis (Priority: P1)

A visitor reads the blog index, opens an article, and can share it from a
single share row at the top of the article (layout governed by spec 002).

**Independent Test**: Open both live articles; share controls present at
the top; article body renders in the left-justified editorial column.

### User Story 3 - Course participant downloads materials (Priority: P2)

An AI 101 participant opens Documents and downloads the current deck and
handout PDFs, which mirror the shared-documents repository automatically.

**Independent Test**: Push a documents change to AXIOVEX/shared-documents;
within one sync cycle the new file is live at /documents/files/ with the
manifest's "Updated" date advanced.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

- **FR-001**: Pages: `/`, `/blog/`, `/blog/<slug>/`, `/contact/`,
  `/documents/`, `/privacy/`, plus the `/blog/post?p=<slug>` redirect shim
  (keeps the 2026-09-30 LinkedIn link working).
- **FR-002**: Page structure and layout conform to
  `docs/wireframes/wireframes.md` WF-01..WF-09 + WF-G1..G4 as approved
  2026-10-04 (and later approved revisions).
- **FR-003**: The site is generated by `scripts/build-site.mjs`
  (dependency-free, byte-deterministic) from `scripts/templates/` and
  content sources; generated pages are never hand-edited.
- **FR-004**: Documents mirror AXIOVEX/shared-documents per its `docs.json`
  manifest; the GitHub Action `site-sync.yml` rebuilds on blog/script
  pushes, manual dispatch, repository_dispatch, and a 15-minute poll.
- **FR-005**: Blog articles are authored as front-matter markdown in
  `blog/posts/` and generated to static pages with sitemap inclusion.
- **FR-006**: Design system per constitution §II (palette, centered axis,
  editorial column exception, founder wording, name usage).
- **FR-007**: Terminology: AI names today's technology; SI is defined as
  superintelligence (ANI → AGI → ASI ladder) in the FAQ (visible +
  FAQPage JSON-LD), llms.txt, and the terminology article.
- **FR-008**: SEO/AEO health monitored weekly + on push against baseline
  (AEO 100/100, Seobility 91) per the monitoring runbook; safe technical
  fixes may auto-apply; claims/branding changes always surface to Tristen.
- **FR-009**: No contact form and no invented proof anywhere on the site;
  contact routes are the published mailboxes (start@, legal@).

## Open risks (tracked, not hidden)

- **R-1**: Compliance/security/accessibility audit is incomplete: privacy
  assertions vs actual Cloudflare behavior, security headers, WCAG checks,
  and substantiation of government/defense-adjacent copy remain open.
  These are tasks in this spec's tasks.md, and no compliance claim may be
  strengthened until they close.
