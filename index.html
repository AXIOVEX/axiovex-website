About

Difficult problems, solved — whatever it takes.

Axiovex Systems is a Michigan company founded by Tristen Pierson and Rockson Arnold. We’re here to solve difficult problems and build connections — doing whatever it takes to make sure problems get solved, and that tomorrow is better than today.

Our approach is to design epistemic structures around a problem: analyze it rigorously, make what is known — and what isn’t — explicit, then build the framework that carries the solution through. Our work starts with education, because tools change fast and judgment compounds.

Axiovex is axio — from axiom, a foundational truth — and vex, from the Latin vexare: to shake, to agitate, to trouble. The name is the method. We shake the axioms — questioning the foundational assumptions until what is actually true falls out — then build on that ground. Every engagement starts there.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Straight answers on AI, training, compliance readiness, and how we work.

What does Axiovex Systems do?

We’re an expert AI consultancy. We train workforces through hands-on courses like AI 101: Beyond the Chatbot, build private AI infrastructure for manufacturers, and design evidence and decision systems — all through our structural epistemic framework, an out-of-the-box approach that questions foundational assumptions and builds from what’s actually true. When time is the problem, we move fast too: rapid-response engineering is part of how we work.

Do you offer AI training for manufacturing teams?

Yes — it’s where our work usually starts. AI 101: Beyond the Chatbot takes teams past the demos to real, everyday capability with modern AI tools. The full slide deck and one-page handout are openly shared: browse the course materials.

What is SI — and how is it different from AI?

SI is superintelligence: a theoretical system smarter than the best human minds in practically every field. It doesn’t exist. Today’s tools — including everything we deploy and teach — are AI: narrow systems that are very good at bounded tasks. You’ll hear “superintelligence” used loosely for today’s products; we don’t use it that way. Our materials teach the ladder — narrow AI, general AI (AGI), superintelligence (SI) — and use each term only for its rung. Why we bother.

How can AI help with CMMC compliance?

CMMC — the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification — is the Department of Defense’s cybersecurity certification for defense contractors. AI can’t certify you, and we aren’t assessors — but it can shoulder the readiness work: organizing evidence, drafting and maintaining policies, tracking remediation, and building the audit trails assessors ask to see. Our evidence-centered systems background maps directly onto that job.

We’re a small manufacturer. Where do we start with AI?

Start with education, then one contained pilot. We typically begin with AI 101 for your team, then pick a single high-value workflow — quoting, quality documentation, maintenance triage — and put a private, measured AI system on it before expanding. Small, proven, then bigger.

How do you handle our data?

It stays yours. We design for private inference: your data stays on your floor, under your control, processed locally — not shipped to third-party clouds. We don’t train shared models on client data. Our website privacy policy covers how we handle information collected through this site.

Do you work on jobs and education pipelines?

Yes — workforce development is core to our mission. Beyond AI 101, we build audited workforce intelligence and work with educators and workforce organizations to connect training pipelines to real manufacturing jobs. Tools change fast; judgment compounds — we help both grow.

Do you work with government or defense organizations?

We’re members of the Macomb Regional APEX Accelerator and of Velocity — the MEDC SmartZone Business Incubator, Accelerator, and International Landing Zone serving Macomb County, Michigan — and we support organizations pursuing government contracts, including CMMC readiness, evidence organization, and AI systems for regulated, high-consequence environments.

What are evidence and decision systems?

Systems that make what is known — and what isn’t — explicit: the evidence, the assumptions, the uncertainty, and the criteria behind a decision. We build audited workforce intelligence and evidence-centered analysis so organizations decide on facts instead of vibes. Our public Michigan workforce research is an example of the approach.

Do you build custom software, or only advise?

Both. We advise — training, evidence-centered analysis, technical strategy — and we build: private AI infrastructure (inference planning, GPU sizing, local AI routing), prototypes, and embedded engineering inside your team. The mix depends on the problem.

What is workforce intelligence?

The practice of turning labor-market evidence into decisions: which skills are genuinely in demand, which training pipelines connect to real jobs, where the gaps are. We build audited workforce intelligence systems, and we publish our Michigan workforce research openly so educators, employers, and policymakers can use it.

Where is Axiovex Systems located?

We’re a Michigan company, rooted in Macomb County. We work with manufacturers and organizations facing high-consequence problems — the kind where getting the answer right matters more than getting it fast.

Who founded Axiovex Systems?

Axiovex Systems was founded in September 2026 by Tristen Pierson (Co-Founder & President) and Rockson Arnold (Co-Founder & Chief of Strategy).
