2. Data from primary sources

Most figures on the Site are republished from primary sources — principally the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, the U.S. Census Bureau, Michigan state agencies, and federal agency publication feeds. Each figure carries its source and the period it describes (its vintage). Publishing agencies revise their data after first release, sometimes substantially, so a figure here reflects the release available when the page was built and may no longer match the source’s current release. Where a number matters to a decision, check the source it came from.

Our own commentary is limited to what the published data does and does not show.
