# Remaining operations — owner direction October 19, 2026

Owner: “fix the rest of these” following the migration audit.

The data-ready monthly release will also publish a deterministic, source-linked
workforce data brief and queue matching personal/company LinkedIn copy. This
is standing authorization for published source facts and checked arithmetic.
It does not invent causal conclusions, a research review, or a new reviewed
research edition. Optional interpretive enrichment remains separate.

LinkedIn uses its OAuth-authorized Posts API. Personal posting precedes the
company reshare; the company step waits until the following Tuesday at 09:30
Eastern. Credentials, product approval and authorized author identities must
be verified. Durable pre-send reservation prevents replay after uncertainty.

Annual source checking runs in CI, records primary-source evidence, detects
changed vintages and safely refreshes supported structured data. Unrecognized
schema changes fail and produce an actionable review item, never stale-as-new.

Newsletter CI covers the browser and real function lifecycle in isolation,
using Cloudflare's published test keys and captured delivery. Live checks
retain the actual managed challenge and mailbox evidence requirements; a
challenge or unavailable mailbox access is explicitly blocked, never passed.
No production anti-bot bypass is introduced.
