Axiovex Systems — Redesign Wireframes

Axiovex redesign — wireframes

Each page of axiovexsystems.com re-structured on a consistent pattern language — sticky nav with CTA, strata bar, premise → services grid → proof → people → CTA band → three-column footer — rendered in Axiovex navy / cyan and the current Axiovex font stack. Dashed boxes are layout blocks; the small cyan labels name each block. Nothing on the live site changes until every wireframe here is approved.

Five adaptations baked into these wireframes — flag anything you want changed

  • 1 · Centered axis kept. Heroes and page heads follow your standing direction — full symmetry: Axiovex keeps its centered titles and one center axis; the pattern language contributes structure, rhythm, and components, never left alignment.
  • 2 · Strata bar = 3 segments. The bar has 3 segments for the 3 services, in cyan-family tints.
  • 3 · No contact form. Our privacy policy states the site has no forms, and there’s no form backend — so the contact wireframe uses a two-column layout whose left column is email routes (start@ / legal@), not a form.
  • 4 · No invented proof. A track-record band would normally carry stats and brand logos; Axiovex has none published, so that band becomes “Evidence in practice” — the latest published analysis plus the AI 101 materials. Real content only.
  • 5 · FAQ stays. The FAQ remains, restyled as clean rows inside the new rhythm.
