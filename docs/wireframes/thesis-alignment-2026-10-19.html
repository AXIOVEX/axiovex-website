The method

Shared constraints. Clear choices. Accountable decisions.

A proposed engine for feasible plans

Our projected constraint engine (PCE) is being developed to compare plans under skills, timing, budget and capacity limits, explain unmet requirements and re-evaluate affected plans when conditions change.

Production guarantees and comparative advantage require validation. We will compare the engine with conventional optimization, simple matching and human coordination using the same inputs and objectives.

Evidence before commitments

Public evidence and approved partner feeds inform each plan. Forecast demand stays separate from funded openings; reported capacity stays separate from confirmed availability.

Students and workers choose their routes. Institutions approve programs, employers hire and companies approve transactions. Shared resources require confirmed reservations or renewed checks.

Questions

What a pilot needs to work.

What does Axiovex Systems do?

We are developing constraint-based planning and coordinated services for education and employment, company resource sharing, and founder attraction in Michigan. Engagements begin with paid qualification and a fixed scope; training and technical delivery are budgeted separately where needed.

Is the projected constraint engine production-ready?

The PCE is a proposed mechanism under development. Production projection guarantees and comparative advantage require validation against conventional solvers, simple matching and competent human coordination. Published research does not by itself establish readiness for every pilot.

Who is the first pilot for?

The proposed education and employment pilot starts with one occupation family, one institution, 3–5 Macomb-area employers and 20–30 participants across student, graduate and incumbent-worker pathways. These are planning targets; launch requires confirmed commitments, capacity and financing.

Do you already have partner data or funding?

Access and funding require specific agreements. A documented vendor API does not grant access to a partner’s records. We start with named data owners, permitted uses and approved samples or exports; automated feeds follow when they improve a useful decision.

How will results be measured?

Agree a baseline and outcomes before launch. Track adopted education changes, paid starts and retained employment; qualified capacity work and total costs; or verified founder interest and subsequent Michigan activity. Targets are not findings, and an introduction is not a relocation.

Do you offer AI training and engineering?

Yes. AI 101 materials remain openly available, and a defined engagement can include training, private AI infrastructure or engineering. These capabilities support the agreed problem and do not substitute for verified employer demand or participant outcomes.

Are startup and business growth packages available?

We are exploring packages that could combine a website, a corporate operating system for managing day-to-day work, and guidance on relevant Michigan business resources. The aim is to help new startups and existing companies make better use of available support. Scope and pricing are still being developed; program eligibility, access and funding would need to be confirmed for each business.
