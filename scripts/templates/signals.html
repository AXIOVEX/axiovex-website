Signals are headlines from public sources, gathered automatically and filtered to the topics we work in. Inclusion is not endorsement, and Axiovex adds no commentary to them here. Pulse figures are U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics statistics, shown with their reference month. Outlook figures are employment projections published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and the Michigan Center for Data and Analytics, shown with their horizon and vintage; they are those agencies’ modeled outlook, not Axiovex forecasts. Education figures are Michigan CEPI statistics, shown with their vintage. Trend deltas on this page are computed from the published series. The highlights summary is generated at build time from the figures on this page; it states only what those figures show. The detail tables reproduce the committed datasets behind the boards: Michigan occupation projections from the Michigan Center for Data and Analytics, transcribed via the Axiovex workforce study’s September edition (a direct read of MCDA’s tables is pending and will supersede the transcription), and education figures as published by CEPI via MDE (February 2026), MDE (September 2025), and the House Fiscal Agency (January 2026 consensus; pupil membership is a budget estimate, as its source labels it).{{GEO_NOTE}} {{UPDATED_LINE}} See our Disclaimer.
