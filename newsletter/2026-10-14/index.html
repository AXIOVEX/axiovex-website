Why it matters

Two Michigan WARN filings landed October 8: Lear Corporation's announced Sterling Heights closure (67 jobs, effective November 9) and HealthEdge / UST HealthProof's announced Detroit layoff (53 jobs, effective December 1). Announced is not completed — both are notices with effective dates still ahead, and this series tracks them as such.

August's Michigan figures — the newest published — show manufacturing employment up 1,100 on the month to 586,700 while the unemployment rate ticked up a tenth of a point to 5.0%: gains and softening in the same release. September's state figures, out October 20, will arbitrate which way the trend is actually moving.

Manufacturing Dive reports thousands of manufacturing layoffs were announced nationally in September. How much of that national total is Michigan is unexplained in the source — the Michigan-specific record this week is the two WARN filings above, not the national headline.
