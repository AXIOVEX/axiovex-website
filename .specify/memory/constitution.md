# Axiovex Systems Website Constitution

Ratified: 2026-10-05 (codifies the standing rules the site was built under).

## I. Claims discipline

- Nothing is published that cannot be substantiated: no invented
  certifications, compliance status, partnerships, statistics, reviews, or
  awards. Where evidence is incomplete, copy is narrowed — never padded.
- Website hygiene (headers, meta, scores) is never presented as
  organizational compliance (GDPR, CMMC, or otherwise).

## II. Brand

- Public name is **Axiovex Systems**; "Axiovex Systems, LLC" appears only
  where legally appropriate (footer legal line, privacy policy, document
  copyright lines).
- Palette: deep navy `#081F32`, electric cyan `#00DEF6`, near-white
  `#F7FCFF`. The logo is never recolored, distorted, or redrawn.
- Design axis is centered and symmetric — "very neat and pristine" — except
  article body pages, which keep a left-justified editorial column
  (~760px) with a centered title block.
- Founders are named as **Tristen Pierson** and **Rockson Arnold**,
  co-founders. Public copy never states ownership percentages or
  "equal partners."

## III. The wireframe gate

- Every UX-impacting change starts in `docs/wireframes/`: wireframes are
  updated first, approved by Tristen, and only then implemented.
- Wireframes are kept always current with the approved live design.

## IV. Generated site integrity

- Pages are generated by `scripts/build-site.mjs` from `scripts/templates/`
  and content sources. Generated output is never hand-edited.
- Stylesheets ship under versioned filenames (cache-busting); every visual
  release bumps the version.
- Documents and blog updates flow through the sync automation
  (shared-documents → site), never manual uploads.

## V. Verification

- Every release is verified live (HTTP status, content, rendering) before
  it is reported done.
- SEO/AEO health is monitored against the recorded baseline (AEO 100/100,
  Seobility 91); safe technical fixes may auto-apply — marketing claims,
  founder wording, and branding never change to satisfy a checker.
